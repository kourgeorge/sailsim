Ten places. Real geography.

The sailing regions use elevation data and mapped coastlines, with scenery inspired by photographs of each place. Buildings, vegetation, roads, traffic, and harbor layouts are simplified interpretations. Sandy Spit has a detailed coastline and a low sand profile interpreted from reference photographs.

Regional terrain samples are approximately 39–83 metres apart. Depths are modeled for the simulator and are not nautical soundings. These scenes are for virtual sailing, not real-world navigation.
