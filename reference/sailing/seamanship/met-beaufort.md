## Original research notes

- The Beaufort table relates wind-force categories to wind-speed ranges and descriptive sea conditions.
- Its wave-height descriptions are contextual estimates, not a promise for every fetch, tide, or coastline.

## Application and limits

The simulator uses wind speed in knots. Its decorative waves do not reproduce the Beaufort table or certify conditions.
