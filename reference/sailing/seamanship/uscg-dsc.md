## Original research notes

- DSC is a maritime radio calling system with distinct distress, urgency, safety, and testing procedures.
- Its official resources distinguish equipment classes and operational procedures.

## Application and limits

The app contains no functioning radio or distress transmitter. Learn the fitted equipment and local procedures before departure.
