# Know What To Do When the Weather Changes

Publisher: NOAA National Weather Service

Source: https://www.weather.gov/safety/safeboating-during

Retrieved: 2026-10-01T00:49:02.552182+00:00

## Original research notes

- Fog, thunderstorms, rapid wind shifts, and other weather changes can make boating dangerous.
- Conditions can change during a trip; knowing the appropriate response is part of preparation.

## Application and limits

The rendered sky and waves do not model all these hazards. A calm-looking scene is not evidence of safe real conditions.
