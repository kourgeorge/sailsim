# What is the difference between a nautical mile and a knot?

Publisher: NOAA National Ocean Service

Source: https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/nautical-mile-knot.html

Retrieved: 2026-10-01T00:49:02.552182+00:00

## Original research notes

- The international nautical mile is exactly 1.852 kilometres.
- A knot expresses speed in nautical miles per hour, rather than distance.

## Application and limits

The physics conversion is 1852/3600 metres per second per knot. This unit reference does not validate vessel resistance or sail coefficients.
