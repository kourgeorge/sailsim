## Original research notes

- Tidal water movement produces flood and ebb currents.
- Current changes direction and speed through the tidal cycle; the source discusses their astronomical drivers.

## Application and limits

The app provides only a spatially uniform, steady current set by the user. Heading and course over ground can differ; no tidal-stream prediction is implemented.
