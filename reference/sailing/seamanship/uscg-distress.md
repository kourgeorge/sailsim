## Original research notes

- Distress procedures involve the radio equipment, the vessel identity and position, and follow-up communications.
- The page explains cancellation of a false alert and monitoring VHF channel 16 after receiving a DSC distress call.

## Application and limits

Never transmit a live distress alert for this exercise. The lesson concerns priorities and information, not equipment certification or rescue competence.
