## Original summary

Yachtmaster Offshore is a practical certificate-of-competence examination, not simply an attendance course. Its scope includes passages up to 150 miles from harbour with day/night and varied weather demands. The ordinary eligibility route lists 50 days, five skipper days, 2,500 miles and five passages over 60 miles, with specified overnight and skipper experience; the page gives additional tidal, discipline, age, radio and first-aid conditions and exceptions. Consult the source for an actual application.

## Proposed learning outcomes

- Distinguish a theory result, a simulated task and an independent practical examination.
- Identify why sea time and emergency preparation cannot be replaced by a badge.
- Plan continued training against a recognized provider’s actual prerequisites.
