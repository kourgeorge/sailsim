## Original summary

The page explains recognized offshore-survival courses delivered through national authorities against World Sailing minimum standards. It distinguishes sailing-specific personal-survival preparation from generic commercial-ship training and notes race-organizer acceptance. Its US section requires theoretical modules plus in-person hands-on work for the described World Sailing-compliant certificate. This is direct evidence that online knowledge alone does not satisfy that practical survival qualification.

## Proposed learning outcomes

- Separate learning emergency priorities from demonstrating survival equipment use.
- Identify the need for hands-on training and current provider requirements.
- Include crew capabilities and emergency preparation in passage decisions.

## Application and limits

Use sail-33–34 early and revisit in Advanced. The app should never issue an offshore-survival credential or count button presses as physical rescue.
