## Original summary

The five-day practical course prepares a skipper for short daytime trips in familiar waters aboard a cruising yacht. Entry experience includes five days aboard, 100 miles and four night hours, plus basic sailing and navigation knowledge at Day Skipper shorebased level. The public description includes pilotage, navigation, handling under sail and power, engines, weather, rules, emergencies and passage making. These prerequisites and onboard assessment prevent equivalence with a browser course.

## Proposed learning outcomes

- Prepare a vessel, crew and day passage.
- Connect pilotage, weather and handling decisions.
- Explain where supervised handling and logged experience are needed.

## Application and limits

Sail-17–36 and the engine lab support selected decisions and model-based handling. They do not establish Day Skipper competence or satisfy sea time.
