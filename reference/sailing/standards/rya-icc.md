## Original summary

RYA explains the UK ICC as evidence of competence under UNECE Resolution No. 40 and describes its authority to issue it for the UK government. Categories concern vessel type and coastal and/or inland waters; they are not a universal Basic/Intermediate/Advanced ladder. Eligibility, evidence and acceptance depend on the issuing process and applicable flag/coastal-state legislation. A shorebased certificate alone does not establish every vessel category. The primary UNECE endpoints returned HTTP 403 during this research, so this is a verified issuing-authority explanation, not a direct verification of the resolution text.

## Proposed learning outcomes

- Distinguish an app learning level from an ICC category.
- Check flag/coastal-state law and receiving-country acceptance for a real voyage.
- Seek current issuing-authority advice for eligibility and practical assessment.
