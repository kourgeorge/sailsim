## Original summary

The public standard combines demonstrated knowledge with practical command of a simple daysailing keelboat in familiar waters and light-to-moderate conditions. It includes preparation, lifejackets, rigging, knots, halyards and sail controls, crew communication, departure and return, steering, getting out of irons, heaving-to, reefing and person-overboard recovery. Navigation rules and local aids also appear. Even an introductory recognized standard extends beyond steering and trim in this simulator.

## Proposed learning outcomes

- Operate modeled sail and helm controls with deliberate sequencing.
- Explain lookout, crew and recovery responsibilities before an exercise.
- Identify omitted practical skills such as knots, heaving-to and physical recovery.

## Application and limits

Grounds Basic sail-01–16, with cross-level review of 18, 25–27 and 33–34. Basic is a learning segment, not equivalence to this certification.
