## Original summary

This standard follows Basic Keelboat and covers an auxiliary-powered cruising yacht by daylight within sight of land in moderate conditions. It adds vessel systems inspections, crew briefing, VHF, navigation and local regulations; power handling includes windage, prop walk, spring lines and controlled arrivals. It also requires sail controls, reefing, rescue, anchoring and emergency responses. Published practical outcomes explicitly include stopping, tying up and securing the boat.

## Proposed learning outcomes

- Perform a structured systems and crew preparation discussion.
- Compare through-water control with ground movement.
- Plan an arrival, abort and emergency response using vessel-specific information.

## Application and limits

Sail-17–36 plus three engine drills cover only part of this material. Open-water stopping is not docking; missing contact, line forces and crew assessment must remain explicit.
