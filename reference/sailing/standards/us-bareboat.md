## Original summary

Following Basic Cruising, this standard concerns an auxiliary cruiser within sight of land and anchoring by daylight. The detailed public outcomes cover charter and systems preparation, crew responsibilities, power handling, fixes and multileg navigation, reefing and heaving-to, rescue and mooring/anchor techniques. Knowledge includes tide, current, leeway, IALA regions, electronic limitations, weather changes and failures. This is evidence for progressive planning complexity, not a synonym for unrestricted offshore readiness.

## Proposed learning outcomes

- Create a passage plan that includes navigation, systems and contingency checks.
- Explain the effect of current, tide and leeway on a plan.
- Assess options after steering, engine or weather changes.

## Application and limits

Use in Intermediate-to-Advanced transfer discussions and sail-37–40. Physical boat handling, seaworthiness checks and charter requirements remain external.
