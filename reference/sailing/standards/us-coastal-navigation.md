## Original summary

This classroom navigation standard has no prerequisite and a written examination. It integrates traditional and electronic methods: charts and publications, bearings, true/magnetic references, variation/deviation, tide and current, dead reckoning and estimated positions, fixes, danger bearings, transits, weather and electronic limitations. A navigation-theory subject can be advanced within this app without implying the learner has advanced seamanship or practical certification.

## Proposed learning outcomes

- Calculate simple current-adjusted ground speed and passage time.
- Keep depth datum and tidal height separate in clearance arithmetic.
- Cross-check electronic position against independent observations.

## Application and limits

Primary source for sail-38, 39 and 42. Fictional numerical examples assess reasoning only and must state assumptions, units and uncertainty.
