## Original summary

The Offshore Special Regulations govern offshore racing and address structural characteristics, yacht and personal equipment, and training. They are sporting requirements, not general navigation law or a universal recreational qualification. The page identifies the 2026–2027 edition as effective from 1 January 2026 and separately a Version 2 effective from 1 January 2027. At the October 2026 review date, the future version must not be described as already effective.

## Proposed learning outcomes

- Distinguish race-entry requirements from local law and competence training.
- Explain why equipment and training requirements depend on the event and conditions.
- Verify the effective version before applying a regulation.
