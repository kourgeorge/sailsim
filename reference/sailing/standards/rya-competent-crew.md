## Original summary

This introductory five-day practical course accepts beginners and develops useful crew participation. It covers yacht language, rig and sails, ropework, safety equipment, fire precautions, person-overboard response, weather awareness, helmsmanship and general duties. Learning occurs aboard a yacht; an interactive screen does not supply the physical experience or assessment.

## Proposed learning outcomes

- Identify equipment and communicate clearly with the crew.
- Explain preparation and emergency roles before departure.
- Transfer sail handling, steering and ropework to supervised onboard practice.

## Application and limits

Use sail-01–16 as preparation, with immediate review links to sail-33–34. Crew communication, knots, loaded lines and physical recovery remain on-water tasks.
