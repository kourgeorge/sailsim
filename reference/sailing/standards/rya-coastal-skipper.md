## Original summary

This advanced five-day practical course develops coastal skippering by day and night. It expects handling at Day Skipper standard and prior experience of 15 days, including two as skipper, 300 miles and eight night hours. Published content includes passage preparation, pilotage, responsibility for crew, handling under power and sail, adverse conditions and emergencies. The course is practical, so written scenarios alone cannot demonstrate the published standard.

## Proposed learning outcomes

- Build a passage plan with contingencies and crew responsibilities.
- Adapt a plan to weather, darkness and restricted options.
- Recognize limits requiring further instruction or an early diversion.

## Application and limits

Provides direction for sail-37–42. Night and adverse-weather decisions are theory only until credible environmental and assessment systems exist.
