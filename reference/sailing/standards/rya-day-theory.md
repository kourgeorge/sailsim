## Original summary

The public description presents roughly 40 hours plus examination time of shorebased preparation for inexperienced skippers. Topics include seamanship, coastal navigation and pilotage, chartwork and electronic charts, position fixing, course to steer, weather, tides and collision regulations. Practical experience is desirable. This confirms that navigation knowledge is assessable independently, while practical boat handling remains a separate learning requirement.

## Proposed learning outcomes

- Explain the purpose and limitations of chart and instrument information.
- Calculate a passage estimate and identify missing tidal information.
- Use forecast and collision-risk information in a conservative plan.

## Application and limits

Sail-21–28 introduce these subjects. Sail-37–42 add bounded decision examples; six short lessons do not cover the full shorebased syllabus.
