Minimum System Requirements

Operating Systems

Windows (10 or above), Mac (Catalina or above)

Processor

2.5 GHz. (On Mac eSail will run on Intel or M1 chip)

Graphics

A dedicated graphics card is normally required. However a higher speed CPU (running at 3.5GHz for instance) with modern integrated Intel graphics should also work at lower quality settings.

Memory

4GB (8GB recommended)

Storage

4GB available space

*What is Steam?

Steam is software which you download and install onto your PC or Mac which helps with the downloading and management of games and other software. There is no extra charge for Steam.
