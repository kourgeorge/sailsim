eSail Carbon Offset – World Land Trust

(Jun 6th 2021) eSail Ltd has offset all estimated carbon emissions for both players and development since 2014.

We have chosen to donate to the

World Land Trust

, an organisation backed by Sir David Attenborough, Steve Backshall, David Gower and Chris Packham. The World Land Trust operates across the world from Argentina to Zambia, and is dedicated to preserving natural forest habitats.

Our donation enables the trust to facilitate the purchase of three acres of forest, protecting the land for wildlife in perpetuity. To date 2,351,275 acres of land has been purchased this way!

Click here

if you too would like to buy an acre or donate.
