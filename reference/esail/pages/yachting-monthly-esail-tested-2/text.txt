Yachting Monthly: eSail Tested

(14 Nov 2018) eSail Sailing Simulator featured in the Tested section of Yachting Monthly December 2018 issue.

​

” VERDICT: There is a vast amount to explore and learn in the simulator – plenty to fill the long cold boat-less winter. It might mean spending time on a computer, but the thought processes and actions required to sail your virtual boat are remarkably close to the real world, so the old grey cells are going through the same processes.”

“It was realistic enough to be genuinely engaging and enjoyable to use, with the element of competition through the online leaderboards helping to motivate you to improve.”

“Now beginners can learn in a fun game like way.. Experienced sailors will find the realistic simulation and competitive challenges addictive.”
