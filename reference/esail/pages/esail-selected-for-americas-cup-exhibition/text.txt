eSail selected for America’s Cup Exhibition

(August 18th 2023) eSail is very proud to have been chosen to feature at the Western Australian Maritime Museum in Fremantle, Australia to celebrate the 40th anniversary of the Australia II America’s Cup Victory. The exhibition will take place from Saturday 9th September 2023 to Sunday 4th February 2024. The Maritime Museum is the official home of the Australia II yacht with the museum being purpose built to house this Western Australian icon.

The sailing yacht and race trophy, alongside other victorious memorabilia, will be on display during the exhibition. eSail will be alongside to help inspire the next generation of sailors to learn to sail.

We are deeply honoured to be involved in such a momentous occasion and hope any eSailors in the area can attend.

The Museum of Western Australia
