New esail v2 planned for 2021

A new version of eSail will be launched in 2021. eSail V2 will have many exciting new features including.

A new asymmetric spinnaker. This is deployed using a snuffer (or sock). It’s great for downwind racing!

A sandbox mode where you can set wind direction, wind strength and wave height. You will also be able to read off your heel and other data.

Multiplayer mode including races and other challenges such as Point of Interest (POI) hunts. You will also be able to build your own challenges for other to enjoy!

Prop walk, so you can really test your mooring skills.

Version 2 will be available free to all eSail V1 users.
